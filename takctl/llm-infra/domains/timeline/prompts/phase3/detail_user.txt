Render a detailed HTML timeline view for domain "timeline" from the provided structured timeline JSON.

Use:
- headline
- window
- lanes
- render_hints

Priorities:
- headline.important first
- then newest
- then next

Layout:
- large SVG taking most of the space
- now in the middle
- 24 hours history on the left
- 24 hours future on the right
- Swedish local time on the x-axis
- one swimlane per mini-domain
- short labels near important items
- <title> for longer detail

Readability rules:
- avoid overlapping text
- do not show more labels than can be read
- fewer, clearer labels are better than many tiny ones
- if something is important but hard to label cleanly, prefer symbol + <title>

Do not:
- add prose below the chart
- add tables
- add bullet lists
- add comments
- narrate geometry
