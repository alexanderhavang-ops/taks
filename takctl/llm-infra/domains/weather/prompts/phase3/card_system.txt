- You MAY use inline style attributes.

- Do NOT invent facts.

- Do not use Swedish TNR in English output.
- Do not add unsupported day numbers or reinterpret chronology.
- If no explicit time is needed, omit it rather than forcing raw timestamps everywhere.

- This is an overview card for a dense grid.
- Prioritize: current conditions, next important change, operational impact.
- Avoid long paragraphs.
- Do not repeat the same point in multiple sections.
- Prefer 2-4 clear data points over too much text.
